# Other worlds

Off the ring, through gates: Mars, Titan, Enceladus, a platform on a gas giant, Venus, and alien worlds. Each is a small
world of its own, walked round in minutes, with its own sky, light, ground and life. PoetryPress paints the same list
as still scenes of its own (Ruby, 2026-10-26); the two projects build them separately.

## The worlds

- **Mars.** Rust dunes and dust devils; the blue sunset Mars really has; Phobos and Deimos racing across the sky;
  pressure domes and rovers; terraced canyon gardens under glass; frost on the rocks at dawn.
- **Titan.** Orange haze with no shadows at noon; methane lakes and rain falling slowly in low gravity; dunes of dark
  grains; Saturn hidden behind the haze.
- **Enceladus.** White plains striped with blue tiger-stripe rifts; geysers lit against a black sky with Saturn huge;
  ice caves glowing blue; the ocean's light coming up through the cracks; once in a long while, on an ice ridge, a
  seven-tailed fox of light, the moon's guardian.
- **A platform on a gas giant.** A built deck in the clouds: rails, masts, docking arms, wind; the bands of the planet
  below and its storms; moons in the sky.
- **Venus.** Cloud cities floating in golden haze; airships between them; the sun a bright smudge; sulphur-yellow rain
  that never reaches the ground.
- **Alien worlds.** By the seed: other suns' light colours, strange life, and stranger rules (the candy world, the
  eldritch valley where rain falls upward).

## How they come in

- **Gates are places on the ring.** A ring of standing stones, glass pylons, or a builders' arch, one per region.
  Its destination's seed is its address (a hash of the ring's seed and the gate's id), so a gate always leads to the
  same world. A typed seed phrase could dial one.
- **Mars, Niven's way, too:** a "Map of Mars" on the ring itself, a raised plateau of thin red air reached by climbing.
- **Scale through the gate**, as Minecraft's Nether is 8:1: a whole moon walked round in minutes.
- **Gates as structure**, borrowing one of two rules: Oblivion's (more open as the story goes on) or Morrowind's
  (find each gate's mark to unlock it).
